DG FISMA holds the bulk of the sustainable-finance files tracked on this site: it leads the CSRD and the EU Taxonomy review, technically oversees the ESRS through EFRAG, and leads the "SFDR 2.0" revision. It has also gained weight within the CSDDD during the Omnibus phase, alongside DG JUST.
