DG ENV leads the development of the PPWR, including the calendar of delegated and implementing acts running through 2030. It is the directorate responsible for turning the Regulation into applicable technical rules, a process that has already suffered at least one visible delay: the implementing act on harmonised labelling, pending since August 2026.
