The Council presidency rotates among member states every six months. Three successive presidencies form a "trio" that agrees a shared 18-month programme, then each member state adds its own six-month priorities on top. The presidency chairs meetings, sets agendas, and acts as an honest broker trying to land compromise texts, it does not itself propose legislation, that stays with the Commission.

**Current trio (1 July 2026 – 31 December 2027):** Ireland, Lithuania, Greece.
**Previous trio (1 January 2025 – 30 June 2026):** Poland, Denmark, Cyprus.

### Presidency-by-dossier

What each presidency has actually landed, or failed to land, on the files tracked on this site.

**Poland (Jan–Jun 2025)**
Agreed the Council's negotiating mandate for the CSDDD/CSRD Omnibus I package in June 2025, the foundation the later trilogue was built on. See [CSDDD]({{ '/directives/csddd/' | relative_url }}), [CSRD]({{ '/directives/csrd/' | relative_url }}).

**Cyprus (Jan–Jun 2026)**
The most active presidency on this site's files to date. Closed the Council's negotiating mandate on SFDR 2.0 on 24 June 2026, just before handing over. Ran the final stretch of Council approval on the CSDDD/CSRD Omnibus, giving final approval on 24 February 2026. Circulated successive Data Omnibus compromise texts through May and June, dropping the most contested cookie-consent and AI-legitimate-interest provisions (Articles 88a-c) on 10 June to try to save a mandate, only for Germany to block it on 26 June regardless. Oversaw the Council's final approval of the AI Omnibus on 29 June 2026. See [SFDR]({{ '/directives/sfdr/' | relative_url }}), [CSDDD]({{ '/directives/csddd/' | relative_url }}), [Digital Omnibus]({{ '/directives/digital-omnibus/' | relative_url }}).

**Ireland (Jul–Dec 2026, current)**
Inherited the stalled Data Omnibus mandate from Cyprus. Reporting suggests an influential group of member states is pushing for "a more sensible direction" now that Ireland holds the chair, though no revived mandate had been confirmed as of mid-August 2026. Continuing Council technical examination on the Digital Networks Act and the Energy Omnibus falls within this presidency's term. See [Digital Omnibus]({{ '/directives/digital-omnibus/' | relative_url }}), [Digital Networks Act]({{ '/directives/digital-networks-act/' | relative_url }}), [Energy Omnibus]({{ '/directives/energy-omnibus/' | relative_url }}).

### Working-group and Coreper leads identified so far

Most technical work happens below ministerial level, in Council working parties and Coreper (the ambassadors' committee), before reaching the presidency's political sign-off.

- **WP TELECOM** — technical examination of the Digital Networks Act.
- **TREE.2.B** (energy working party) — technical examination of the Energy Omnibus, began 24–26 June 2026.
- **Antici Group** — an early channel for national objections on the Data Omnibus; France, Poland and Romania raised written objections to folding cookie-consent rules into the GDPR here as early as March 2026.
- **Coreper (ambassador level)** — where the 8 June 2026 failure to agree, and the 26 June 2026 German-led block, on the Data Omnibus mandate played out.
