ECON appointed Gerbrandy rapporteur for the SFDR review on 29 January 2026. His report, published in early May 2026, keeps the Commission's three-category architecture (Sustainable, Transition, ESG Basics) but tightens several elements: it requires "Sustainable" products to specify their principal adverse impacts, adds an obligation to publish a sustainability engagement strategy, and removes certain safeguard mechanisms in favour of more uniform exclusion rules across products.

Shadow rapporteurs include Luděk Niedermayer (EPP, Czechia), Lara Wolters (S&D, Netherlands, the same MEP who led the original CSDDD), Jaroslava Pokorná Jermanová (Patriots for Europe, Czechia), Denis Nesci (ECR, Italy), Kira Marie Peter-Hansen (Greens, Denmark) and Martin Günther (The Left, Germany), a line-up spanning the full political spectrum that points to an especially contested amendments negotiation.
