Wolters led the CSDDD from the Commission's original 2022 proposal through its adoption in 2024, as rapporteur in the Legal Affairs Committee (JURI). When the Omnibus I package reopened the file in 2025, JURI appointed Jörgen Warborn (EPP, Sweden) as rapporteur in her place, reflecting the shift of the file's political centre of gravity toward the EPP in the current mandate. Wolters resigned as S&D's negotiator on the file once the December 2025 Omnibus deal was reached, a sign of how far the final text had moved from her original position.

She now sits as a shadow rapporteur on the SFDR 2.0 review in ECON, alongside members spanning from the EPP to the Left.
