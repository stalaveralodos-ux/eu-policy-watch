August was the month the PPWR stopped being a file to track and became a law companies actually have to follow, and immediately showed the gap between the EU's simplification ambitions and its own delivery record: the Commission missed the labelling deadline on the very day the Regulation started applying. Everything else tracked here moved more quietly, mostly slippage rather than resolution, SFDR's vote, the Taxonomy's second wave, both later than originally planned.
